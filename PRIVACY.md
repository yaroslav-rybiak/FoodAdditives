# Privacy policy

*Food Additives* (Android package `com.yrybiak.foodadditives`)

Last updated: 11 November 2026

## Summary

The app does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no
  crash reporting. It contains no third-party tracking or advertising libraries.
- **No network access.** The app does not request the Internet permission, or any permission
  that gives access to your data, location, camera, contacts or files. All content is bundled
  with the app and works offline. (The only permission in the app is an internal one added by
  the Android support library; it is private to the app and grants access to nothing.)
- **What is stored on your device.** Only your choice of light or dark theme, kept in the
  app's private storage. It never leaves the device and is removed when you uninstall the app.
  If Android's backup is enabled on your device, the system may include this setting in your
  own device backup.
- **Links.** The About page contains an e-mail link. Tapping it opens your own e-mail app;
  the app itself sends nothing.
- **Children.** The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
  children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
